Frequently Asked Questions

How do I log in to my EduVision account? ▼
You can log in using your registered Email Address or 10-digit Mobile Number along with your password. Alternatively, use Google 1-click sign in.
What if I forgot my password? ▼
Please contact your assigned counsellor or the administration support desk to securely verify and reset your access credentials.
Can I track my college admission status live? ▼
Yes! Once logged in, your Student Dashboard displays your active applications, document verification status, Bihar Student Credit Card status, and counsellor notes in real-time.

Absolutely. EduVision uses 256-bit SSL encryption and strict data privacy compliance. Your documents are shared strictly with official university admission departments.
